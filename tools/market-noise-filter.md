---
title: Market Noise Filter
---

This tool does not find good trades.

It highlights conditions where price movement
is likely to mislead participants.

### What it shows
- volatility expansion
- liquidity thinning
- structural risk

### What it does not do
- predict price
- suggest entries
- optimise returns

Use it as a visibility tool, not a signal.
